## 5. Discussion
### 5.1 Project introduction and purpose
- Project name: **Smart-AP-Suite**.
- Purpose: automate the Accounts Payable (AP) flow.

### 5.2 Key functional capabilities
The customer described five capabilities:
1. A user uploads an invoice (PDF or image) to the system manually.
2. The system automatically extracts the information on the invoice.
3. The system automatically performs 1-way, 2-way or 3-way matching, according to three invoice types:
   non-PO invoice, non-trade PO invoice and trade PO invoice.
4. If there are exceptions, the flow pauses and the system sends a notification to an Accounting Member,
   who reviews the invoice and updates it manually.
5. At the end of the flow, an Accounting Member is notified to check the result.

Terms are defined in the [§12 Glossary](../../02-architecture/arc42/12-glossary.md).
